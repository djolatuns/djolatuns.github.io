Simple growth system

  1. Plan: define the audience, goal, deadline and budget.
  2. Create: prepare original content and confirm permissions.
  3. Review: check accuracy, sound/image quality and clear disclosures.
  4. Publish or deliver: use the agreed channel and timeline.
  5. Measure: record what happened and improve the next campaign.

Important: Tools are suggestions, not endorsements. Check each provider's current terms, age requirements, privacy settings and costs. Never upload passwords, private keys or customer payment details into public forms. Use only music, images and other content you own or are authorised to use.
