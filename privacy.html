YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy notice

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Community posts

When you publish in the community, your chosen display name and post text are stored in a shared database and can be viewed by visitors. Do not post private contact details, passwords, school details or sensitive personal information.

Abuse prevention

The community service uses a one-hour posting limit. A hashed version of the network address is stored to apply this limit. The app does not ask for an account or password to post.

Booking enquiries

Booking details are prepared in your own email or WhatsApp app using the public contact details configured by the site owner. This static site does not itself store booking enquiry forms.

External services

Music links may open Audiomack. Booking links may open your email or WhatsApp app. Those services have their own privacy policies.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the site owner through the official DJ Olatuns public contact channel.
