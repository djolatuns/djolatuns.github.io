Effective date: 2 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the DJ OLATUNS app and its related website services handle information when you visit, create an account, use community features, submit a booking request, or enable notifications.

Information we may collect

  • Account information: information you provide to register or sign in, such as your name, email address, and account identifiers.
  • Community content: text posts and information you choose to submit through community features.
  • Booking requests: details you submit to request DJ services, which may include your name, email, phone number, event type, event location, and event details.
  • Push notifications: a device/browser push subscription token if you enable notifications, plus notification delivery status.
  • Technical information: limited information needed to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and maintain the service, which may be processed by our hosting and service providers.

How information is used

Information is used to provide and maintain the app, manage accounts, display community submissions, respond to booking requests, deliver notifications you enable, protect the service against abuse, and communicate about relevant service activity.

Service providers and sharing

The service uses third-party infrastructure providers, including GitHub Pages for website hosting and Supabase for app data and account-related services. Information may be processed by these providers to operate the service. We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect users, the service, or our rights.

Retention and your choices

We keep information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, respond to requests, maintain security, and meet legal obligations. You can choose not to provide optional information or disable push notifications in your device/browser settings. To request access, correction, or deletion of information associated with your account, contact us using the email below. Some records may need to be retained where legally required or for legitimate security purposes.

Children's privacy

The app is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review and take appropriate action.

Security

We use reasonable measures intended to protect information. No online service or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
