About this app

Welcome to the DJ OLATUNS App

The official DJ OLATUNS app brings the brand's music, updates and member community together in one place. Visitors can explore the latest mixtapes, check status updates, read community posts and view official announcements.

Members can create an account or sign in from the Account page. After signing in, members can access the app sections and manage their membership account and phone notifications.

About the brand

Meet DJ OLATUNS

Ismail Olatunji Taiwo, known professionally as DJ OLATUNS, is a Nigerian DJ and mixtape curator with a passion for creativity, entertainment and bringing people together through music. Guided by the motto “Undefeatable,” he is building his identity through energetic mixes, consistency and a commitment to memorable musical experiences.
