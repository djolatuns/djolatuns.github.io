About the DJ OLATUNS App

Welcome to the DJ OLATUNS App

The official DJ OLATUNS app brings music, announcements, bookings and the members community together in one place. Explore releases, keep up with updates, connect with the DJ OLATUNS movement and manage your member account.

The app is designed to work on mobile phones and computers, with an installable app-like experience on supported devices. Members can register or sign in through the Account page and use their account to access the Community posting feature.

Latest app updates

What's new

Text-only Members Community

The Community page has been rebuilt for text posts. Signed-in members can write an optional title, share an update of up to 3,000 characters and publish it to the community feed. Photo and video uploads are not part of this page.

Members Account Centre

Visitors can create an account or sign in, check their account status, manage available account options and control phone notifications for official announcements.

Community feed refresh

The Community feed displays recent posts and includes a refresh option so members can check for the latest updates.

Official announcements and mixtapes

Visit News for official announcements and Mixtapes for DJ OLATUNS music releases and listening links.

Event booking requests

Use the Booking page to send an event enquiry. A request is not a confirmed booking until DJ OLATUNS contacts you and confirms the details.

The person behind the brand

Meet Ismail “DJ OLATUNS” Taiwo

Ismail Olatunji Taiwo, professionally known as DJ OLATUNS, is a Nigerian DJ, mixtape curator and creative builder. He is passionate about music, technology, entertainment and using digital ideas to bring people together.

His DJ identity is built around energetic mixes, creativity, consistency and memorable musical experiences. The name “Olatuns” comes from his third name, Olatunji, and has grown into his public creative identity.

He describes himself as easygoing, gentle, respectful and kind. He values learning from experience, helping people solve problems and sharing what he learns with others.

How the DJ and app are connected

One creator. One official digital home.

This is the official app for the DJ OLATUNS brand, created to bring the DJ, listeners, members and music activities together. It is directly connected to the DJ OLATUNS brand and its creator.

Ismail is the person behind the brand and the platform. He shapes its identity, chooses its features and oversees the official content and member experience. The app gives his audience one central place to discover his work, follow announcements, join the community and send booking enquiries.

The app extends the DJ OLATUNS experience beyond individual social media pages and gives the brand a central digital home it can organise and develop over time.

Purpose and direction

Mission, vision and values

Mission

To share music, creativity and entertainment, build a positive community around DJ OLATUNS, and make it easier for listeners and supporters to connect with the work.

Vision

To grow DJ OLATUNS into a recognised creative and entertainment brand, supported by a strong official app and a connected community.

Core values

  • Creativity: bringing fresh ideas into music and digital experiences.
  • Consistency: continuing to learn, improve and create.

  • Growth: learning through experience and helping others.
