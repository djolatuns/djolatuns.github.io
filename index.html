Meet DJ OLATUNS

Nigerian DJ · Mixtape curator

Ismail Olatunji Taiwo, known professionally as DJ OLATUNS, is a Nigerian DJ and mixtape curator with a passion for creativity, entertainment and bringing people together through music. Guided by the motto “Undefeatable,” he is building his identity through energetic mixes, consistency and a commitment to memorable musical experiences.
