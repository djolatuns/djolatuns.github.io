About DJ OLATUNS

Ismail Olatunji Taiwo known as DJ OLATUNS, Xis a Nigerian DJ, mixtape curator and music enthusiast dedicated to creating exciting musical experiences. Known for the motto “Undefeatable,” he is building a distinctive identity through energetic mixes, creativity and a passion for entertaining audiences. Through projects such as DJ Olatuns 2026 Dance Mix, Undefeatable DJ Olatuns Faaja Mixtape and DJ Olatuns 2026 Movement Mixtape, he continues to develop his sound and connect with music lovers. His upcoming project, New Wave 2026, is another step in his musical journey. Driven by creativity, consistency and a desire to improve, DJ OLATUNS aims to grow his brand, deliver memorable entertainment and bring people together through music.
